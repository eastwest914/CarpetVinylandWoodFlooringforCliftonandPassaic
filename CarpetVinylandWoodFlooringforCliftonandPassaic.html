Before You Pick a Floor

What is the difference between vinyl floor and vinyl sheet?

Vinyl flooring is sold as planks or tiles, while vinyl sheet comes in large rolls that cover a room with few seams. Sheet is a common choice for kitchens and baths.


Is linoleum different from vinyl?

Yes. Linoleum is made from natural materials and is known for durability, while vinyl is a synthetic product. We can walk you through the differences for your room.


Can I buy a carpet and have you install it?

Yes. We sell carpet and install it for you, so one visit covers both the choice and the work.


Do you resurface wood floors?

We do. Wood resurfacing helps tired floors look fresh, depending on their condition.
